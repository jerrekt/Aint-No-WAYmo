    The challenge

    After presenting Opera and emerging technology to our class, guest speaker Dan Berte challenged us to build something that appears to think—in just three weeks.

    We chose an autonomous miniature Waymo: a playful way to put real sensing, control, and engineering tradeoffs into something barely bigger than a toy.

    What we're building

    A self-contained diecast vehicle that can travel between selected room doors, stop for obstacles, identify where it is, and report its decisions.

    Our goal is an indoor, supervised demonstration—not a self-driving car on public roads.

    ESP32-S3: on-car motion and local safety · Kratos: missions, map, and live status

    Powered by Kratos

    Independent Proxmox homelab

    Kratos is an independently operated homelab that hosts websites, game servers, and technical projects—including work by SJSU students on senior projects. For Mini-Waymo, it is planned to run the mission dashboard, map, MQTT communication, and trip history while the car keeps immediate safety decisions onboard.
