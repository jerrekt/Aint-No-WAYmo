Kratos: independently operated home server / homelab.
Virtualization host: Proxmox.
Other hosted uses: websites, game servers, project infrastructure.
Also used by SJSU students for other projects, including senior projects.
Not owned or managed by SJSU.
Mini-Waymo Micro planned services: website, admin/guest access, saved indoor hallway map, route planner, mission queue, MQTT broker, telemetry, and mission history.
Embedded ESP32-S3 retains local motor/sensor control and safety stop.
Deployment status for Mini-Waymo: backend example and simulator exist; live physical deployment unverified.
