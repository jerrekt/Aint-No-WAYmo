The separate 1:43 Waymo model would be ~109 × 47 mm outside; this exact linked model may be a different scale. Opening and measuring the shell is required before ordering the drivetrain.

1. Measure the actual shell

Measure between interior walls, excluding wheel wells, mounting posts, glass, and the actual roof/seat geometry. A rectangular envelope is an optimistic upper bound.

2. Interactive top-down packaging

Drag the colored component rectangles to reposition. Overlaps and out-of-bounds are flagged; wheel wells, solder joints, wires, and component stacking are not yet modeled.

3. Candidate electronics

PartFootprintHeight

XIAO Sense height includes camera expansion-board estimate. Battery dimensions are a placeholder—use an actual protected cell datasheet. Physical clearance must include wires and connector bends.

4. Critical mechanical realities

  • Drive train: two 19 mm long motors opposite each other would span ~38 mm before adapters—likely too wide for a 35 mm cavity. Use a modified micro-RC donor drivetrain or staggered/gear-reduced drive after measurement.
  • Metal shell: may block Wi-Fi; route the antenna toward a plastic window or roof pod and verify reception.
  • Camera: needs a clear outward view; choose a windshield/window or sensor-pod opening. Do not assume transparent windows.
  • Sound: tiny piezo is easiest; faithful recorded chime requires speaker and audio playback space.
  • Safety: include an accessible physical kill switch and a protected LiPo with suitable charging.
  • Chassis: retain original exterior shell, not necessarily original wheel/axle mounting.

5. Export measured envelope

The exported JSON includes outside, inside and component locations; share it after the shell is opened.
